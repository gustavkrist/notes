## What are LLMs

Advanced language models trained on large amounts of text.
Text is turned into tokens and numbers and processed by the model.
The most likely next token is predicted by the model as the next word.
Transformers and self-attention is used to help models keep context.

AI Field

- Artificial Intelligence - Broad field
  - Machine Learning - Learn from data
    - Deep Learning - NN with many layers
      - Generative AI - Creates new content from input
        - LLM - GenAI focused mainly on language with many parameters trained on large amounts of data

Self-attention helps the model decide which tokens in the context are most relevant for the current token.
